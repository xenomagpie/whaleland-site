What we work on

Language learning

We develop tools for language learning, with a focus on practicing new expressions in everyday conversations.

AI agents

We also build AI agents for tasks that need regular attention, such as spotting relevant updates in the sources a reader follows.

Between the two sits information tracking and discovery: keep checking the same source, and find the changes worth telling the person. It shows how an agent can take over a repeated task, and it is the idea behind Zel.Q.

Zel.Q

Zel.Q is a desktop app in development at Whaleland Inc.

How it works

  1. You choose a public website or RSS feed.
  2. You describe the update you care about.
  3. Zel.Q is designed to check that source on a schedule.
  4. When it finds a match, it notifies you, with a link to the original.

For example, you might want to know when a language school opens enrollment for its next evening course. Zel.Q could follow the school’s public course page and send you a link to the announcement.
